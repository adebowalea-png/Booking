💡 Ideas
1. Quick actions on hover
Hover over a time slot to quickly reschedule or cancel without leaving the calendar.
2. Upsell on rescheduling
Let learners choose longer lessons when rescheduling, prompting an upsell if they don't have enough lesson balance.
3. Split lesson on reschedule
Let learners split a long lesson into multiple shorter sessions across different time slots when rescheduling.
4. Cancel one or all lessons
Ask learners if they want to cancel just this lesson or all upcoming lessons.
5. View all lessons in sidebar
Show all scheduled and available lessons in one place so learners can track their balance.
6a. Upcoming lessons: Floating
Add a floating view so learners can check and manage upcoming lessons without leaving the flow.
6b. Upcoming lessons: Sidebar
Show upcoming lessons in a collapsible sidebar section for quick access and management.
6c. Upcoming lessons: Drawer
Open a drawer from the sidebar to view and manage upcoming lessons in one place.
7a. Fix next conflict: Modal
After rescheduling, a modal prompts learners to resolve the next conflict right away or skip for now.
7b. Fix next conflict: Inline
After rescheduling, the next conflict is shown inline on the confirmation screen.
7c. Fix next conflict: On reschedule
While selecting a new time, a modal prompts learners to queue up the next conflict before finishing.
8. Lesson duration visibility
Replace the subtle underlined duration link with a more visible element.
